    Rules that keep it even

    Trim to the thinner side

    Match the outline to your sparser side, never the fuller one. People read symmetry of the edge long before they notice density.

    Cut dry, cut in daylight

    Wet hair looks longer and lies flatter. Always comb out and dry first, and trim facing a window so shadows don't fake a gap.

    Fill a small gap

    A fine-tip brow pencil or beard pen one shade lighter than your hair, flicked in short strokes along the growth direction, reads as real hair.

    Minoxidil, maybe

    It thickens patchy facial hair for some men over 3–6 months, and it isn't for everyone. Ask a doctor or pharmacist before starting.
